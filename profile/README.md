# About Liniantt

**Liniantt** is the parent organization behind a growing collection of focused engineering, technology, and product initiatives.

The name represents:

> **Living In New Innovation And Networked Technological Trends**

Our work spans software engineering, Linux systems, infrastructure, financial technology, media, design, research, developer tooling, and digital products.

Rather than placing every idea or product under one identity, Liniantt provides the umbrella while individual organizations develop their own technical focus, products, communities, and engineering direction.

# Engineering Principles

We aim to build technology that is:

**Purposeful**  
Technology should solve a real problem or enable a meaningful capability.

**Maintainable**  
Systems should remain understandable and operable long after their initial implementation.

**Reliable**  
Engineering decisions should account for failure, recovery, observability, and operational reality.

**Modular**  
Products and platforms should have clear boundaries and evolve without unnecessary coupling.

**Documented**  
Architecture, decisions, systems, and operational knowledge should not exist only in people's heads.

**Evolving**  
Technology changes. Our systems and engineering practices should be able to change with it.
